A **homogeneous transformation matrix (HTM)** is a fundamental concept in robotics used to represent both the rotation and translation of a coordinate frame relative to another. In 3D space, it is represented as a \(4 \times 4\) matrix:

$$
T = \begin{bmatrix} R & d \\ 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix}
$$

Where:

*   \(R\) is a \(3 \times 3\) rotation matrix.
*   \(d\) is a \(3 \times 1\) translation vector.
*   \(0\) is a \(1 \times 3\) zero row vector, and \(1\) is a scalar.
